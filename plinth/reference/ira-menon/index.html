An interiors studio for people who stay a long time.

Ira Menon Interiors designs apartments, houses and a small number of cafés and clinics across Karnataka. We work with lime, local granite, cane and reclaimed teak, and with craftspeople we have known for years.

Most projects take nine to fourteen months. We keep to six at a time, so that Ira reviews every drawing and visits every site.
