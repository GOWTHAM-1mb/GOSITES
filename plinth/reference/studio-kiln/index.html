How we work

  1. Brief

    A two-hour conversation at the site. We write it up and send it back within a week.

  2. Material study

    Physical samples at the studio, never only renderings. Fixed fee.

  3. Drawings

    Full working drawings and a costed schedule before anything is ordered.

  4. On site

    Weekly visits, a shared photo log, and one person responsible from start to finish.
